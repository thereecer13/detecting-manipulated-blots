# Thermo WB upload stamped 2021-09-23: blot-by-blot review

61 western blot images carry the upload stamp `2021923125244`. Per-blot verdicts are in upload_2021-09-23.json.
Review images are in reviews/thermo_controls/ (outside the repo): u0923_flat_canvas.png, u0923_gipr.png,
equalize_demo.png, texture_check.png, cabp5_srrm2_patternA.png and acvrl1_patternB.png.

## Checks
1. **Shared background on raw pixels** (u0923_texture_pairs.py plus a lane-crop variant).
   - Method: high-pass texture with smooth shading, frame lines and bands removed, correlated within each
     layout over a small shift search.
   - Control: every pair is also scored with one image flipped, which gives r ≈ 0.
   - No histogram equalization is used for scoring.
2. **Copied bands.** An automatic band-to-band match was tried, but it was dominated by template elements
   (ladder ticks, +/- signs). The checks that worked were the visual review and the band-shape zooms.
3. **Erased or blank areas.** "Flat" pixels are those whose 7×7 neighbourhood varies by ≤1 grey level and
   that are not pure white or black. A scanned membrane always has some noise, so flat fill inside a
   labelled lane means that lane holds no membrane image.

### Shared background scores
Pattern A uses lane crops, compared pairwise and against the PA5-99645 exemplar:
- EVL, ELF5, SRRM2, CABP5 and USP24: r 0.67–0.82 with each other and 0.52–0.60 with PA5-99645.
- CAV1-3: r 0.44–0.50.
- NKCC1: r 0.52–0.53 with CABP5 and SRRM2.
- SOS2: r 0.12–0.20.
- Flipped controls: median 0.014, max 0.044.

Pattern B, against TSEN54 PA5-101939 and GRB14 PA5-101612 (WB, stamped 2019-08-08):
- PTPN22: r 0.58–0.72.
- PLEIOTROPHIN: r 0.57–0.64.
- ACVRL1: r 0.43–0.44.
- APRIL: r 0.15–0.16.
- Flipped controls: ≤0.06.

Ten blots for ten different targets share one or two backgrounds, and the pattern B one also appears in a
2019 upload. That is not possible if each image is a separate blot.

Only one of the 61 blots is already in Richardson's spreadsheets: PLEIOTROPHIN PA5-118876, listed as
pattern B on 3 June 2026. The other nine confirmed blots and all nine possible ones are not listed.

### Blank canvas and drawn-looking bands
The boxed 2-lane blots are lanes placed on a flat fill (grey 235). In most of them, both lanes still carry
membrane texture. The exceptions:
- **TSC2, P130, EPHB3 and ATP1A1:** the labelled lane 1 has no membrane texture at all. It is fill.
- **COL14A1:** 88% of the box is fill, including most of lane 2.
- **ATAXIN7:** lane 2 has no membrane texture. The band is a uniform bar with a white halo, sitting on the fill.
- **GIPR:** the band is a 2-px hairline crossing a white gap with no membrane, and a lane strip next to it has
  a jagged cut edge.

These are listed as possible, not confirmed. Heavy background removal or splicing could also produce a flat
lane. Even so, the figure shows a lane where no membrane data is shown, and for ATAXIN7 and GIPR the band
itself is not on a membrane image.

### Limits
- The 22 dark peptide-blocking blots have near-black, saturated lanes, so texture scoring was not possible for
  most pairs. Visual review found no shared backgrounds there, apart from the GIPR observation above.
- The automatic copied-band search did not work (template elements dominated).
